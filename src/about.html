About SBA Awards

SBA Awards (Startups Businesses Africa Awards) is a prestigious event that aims at recognizing and rewarding outstanding startups across Africa. Our mission is to celebrate innovation, social impact, and entrepreneurship in the African startup ecosystem.

Each year, we bring together entrepreneurs, investors, and industry leaders to honor the achievements of startups that are driving positive change and making significant contributions to their communities and industries.

At SBA Awards, we believe in the power of startups to transform economies, create jobs, and solve pressing social and environmental challenges. Through our platform, we seek to inspire and empower the next generation of African entrepreneurs to dream big and build impactful businesses.
