SBA Awards (Startups Businesses Africa Awards) is an esteemed event aimed at recognizing and rewarding African startup businesses that demonstrate exceptional innovation, creativity, and impact. By celebrating the entrepreneurial spirit of Africa, SBA Awards strives to inspire and empower the next generation of entrepreneurs, driving economic growth and social development across the continent.
